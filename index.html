Theory of Income · UChicago · Fall 2026 (Alvarez)

Companion Course: Welfare Theorems & Overlapping Generations

20 lessons covering lecture folders 1 (Welfare Theorems) and 2 (OLG Models). Each lesson takes about 30–40 minutes and ends with exercises that have hints and full solutions.
How to use this. Do the lessons in order; each one builds on the last. Attempt every exercise before opening the solution, because effortful recall is what makes it stick. The figures are interactive: drag the points and move the sliders. When you return to a topic, start with the quiz rather than re-reading. The cheatsheets are for quick review before exams. Ask your teacher (Claude) about anything unclear.
