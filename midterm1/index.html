5. Circulating answers that are wrong

Some solution sets and review notes floating around the cohort contain these errors. The guide's model answers avoid them. Know the right version:

  • "The OLG equilibrium is inefficient because the unborn can't trade (incomplete markets)." No. The OLG CE is a complete-markets Arrow–Debreu equilibrium. The 1WT fails because the value of the aggregate endowment can be infinite (double infinity). L12 §5
  • "\(r>0\) in all periods ⇒ efficient." Only without growth. With growth the test is \(1+r\) vs \((1+g)(1+n)\). And even without growth, justify it with the finite-value argument. L13 §5
  • "\(r<0\) ⇒ over-accumulation of capital." In the pure-exchange OLG there is no capital; aggregate saving is zero.
  • 2WT stated as "convexity ⇒ every PO is a CE with transfers". It delivers a quasi-equilibrium; you need a cheaper point (Arrow's remark) and a non-satiated household. L6 §3
  • "PO iff it solves the planner's problem for some \(\lambda\ge0\), \(\lambda\ne0\)." The "if" direction needs \(\lambda\gg0\). L21 §6
  • "The planner's common MRS does not depend on \(\lambda\)." The condition doesn't contain \(\lambda\); its value does. L22 §5
  • Fall 2025 Final Q2.2: \(U=H(\bar e-\sum_i\lambda_i\theta^i)\). Wrong; with equal weights \(U=\lambda H(\bar e-\bar\theta)\). L22 Ex 22.1
  • PSET 1 P4 Q8: "a uniform PAYG tax makes borrowers strictly worse off". Not necessarily: the answer is "not necessarily Pareto superior". L16 §3
  • Diamond: \(r_t=F_K(K_t,1)-\delta\). The dating is \(K_{t+1}\). L15 §2
  • "Lump-sum taxes / IRS / quasi-concavity matter for the 1WT." None of them does. L4 §3
