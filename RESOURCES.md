## General Equilibrium & Welfare Theorems
- **Debreu (1959), *Theory of Value*** (Cowles Monograph 17): free PDF at https://cowles.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-09/m17-all.pdf. The reference cited in Slides 1.
- **Mas-Colell, Whinston & Green, *Microeconomic Theory*** (1995), ch. 15–16: Edgeworth box, CE, welfare theorems, quasi-equilibrium. The standard PhD text.
- **Starr, *General Equilibrium Theory: An Introduction*** (2011): gentler treatment.

## Overlapping Generations
- **Samuelson (1958)**, "An Exact Consumption-Loan Model of Interest…", JPE 66(6): 467–482. https://ideas.repec.org/a/ucp/jpolec/v66y1958p467.html. The original OLG paper.
- **Shell (1971)**, "Notes on the Economics of Infinity", JPE 79(5): 1002–1011. https://ideas.repec.org/a/ucp/jpolec/v79y1971i5p1002-11.html. The "double infinity" explanation of why the 1WT fails.
- **Diamond (1965)**, "National Debt in a Neoclassical Growth Model", AER 55(5): 1126–1150. https://www.jstor.org/stable/1809231. OLG with capital, dynamic inefficiency, debt.
- **Ljungqvist & Sargent, *Recursive Macroeconomic Theory***, OLG chapter: careful notation, many exercises.
- **Krueger, *Macroeconomic Theory* lecture notes** (Penn): chapters on the welfare theorems in infinite economies and pure-exchange OLG; widely circulated PDF.

## Perpetual Youth
- **Blanchard (1985)**, "Debt, Deficits, and Finite Horizons", JPE 93(2): 223–247. https://ideas.repec.org/a/ucp/jpolec/v93y1985i2p223-47.html (NBER WP: https://www.nber.org/papers/w1389). The source of the BY slides and their Figs. 2 and 4.
- **Yaari (1965)**, "Uncertain Lifetime, Life Insurance, and the Theory of the Consumer", REStud 32(2): 137–150. https://academic.oup.com/restud/article-abstract/32/2/137/1555438. Annuities with uncertain lifetimes.

## Aggregation & the Social Planner
- **Mas-Colell, Whinston & Green**, §4.B–4.D (aggregate demand, Gorman form, representative consumer) and §16.E–16.F (Pareto optimality and social welfare optima; first-order conditions).
- **Negishi (1960)**, "Welfare Economics and Existence of an Equilibrium for a Competitive Economy", *Metroeconomica* 12(2–3): 92–97. The weights λ_i = 1/μ_i and Negishi's method.
- **Gorman (1953)**, "Community Preference Fields", *Econometrica* 21(1): 63–80. Linear Engel curves with a common slope.
- **Acemoglu, *Introduction to Modern Economic Growth*** (2009), §5.2: the representative household and Gorman aggregation, in a macro setting.

## Euler Equations & Transversality (folder 4)
- **Stokey & Lucas with Prescott (1989), *Recursive Methods in Economic Dynamics***, §4.5 (Euler equations; Theorem 4.15, sufficiency of EE + TC). The source the slides follow.
- **Acemoglu, *Introduction to Modern Economic Growth*** (2009), ch. 6 (discrete-time dynamic programming, Euler equations, transversality) and ch. 11 (AK model).
- **Kamihigashi (2001)**, "Necessity of Transversality Conditions for Infinite Horizon Problems", *Econometrica* 69(4): 995–1012. When the TC is necessary.
- **Hayashi (1982)**, "Tobin's Marginal q and Average q: A Neoclassical Interpretation", *Econometrica* 50(1): 213–224. Average q equals marginal q under constant returns.

## Local Stability and Linearization (folder 5)
- **Stokey & Lucas with Prescott (1989), *Recursive Methods in Economic Dynamics***, Ch. 6: local stability of optimal trajectories, linearization around the steady state, almost-reciprocal pairs for discrete-time problems. The primary source the slides follow.
- **Blanchard & Kahn (1980)**, "The Solution of Linear Difference Models under Rational Expectations", *Econometrica* 48(5): 1305–1311. Conditions for determinacy (unique stable path) of linear rational-expectations systems — the saddle-point property stated and checked.
- **King, Plosser & Rebelo (2002)**, "Production, growth and business cycles: Technical appendix", *Computational Economics*: linearization of the neoclassical growth model, computation of g′(k*) in practice.

## Continuous-Time Optimal Control and Hamiltonian (folder 7)
- **Pontryagin, Boltyanskii, Gamkrelidze & Mishchenko (1962), *The Mathematical Theory of Optimal Processes***: the original statement of the maximum principle. Historical reference.
- **Acemoglu, *Introduction to Modern Economic Growth*** (2009), Ch. 7: continuous-time optimal control, Hamiltonian, phase diagrams for the neoclassical growth model, linearization and local stability; rigorous and comprehensive.
- **Kamihigashi (2001)**: see Euler equations entry — also applies to continuous-time TC.
- **Anderson, Hansen, McGrattan & Sargent (1996)**, "Mechanics of Forming and Estimating Dynamic Linear Economies", in *Frontiers of Business Cycle Research* (ed. Cooley): thorough LQR treatment with Riccati equation and applications.
- **Stengel (1994), *Optimal Control and Estimation*** (Dover): engineering-oriented LQR reference with accessible proofs of the algebraic Riccati equation.
